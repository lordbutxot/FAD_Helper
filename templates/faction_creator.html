What is a Faction?

Factions are collections that organize your forces:

  • Create thematic armies
  • Group related units together
  • Organize army lists by faction
  • Define faction-wide special rules
  • Share factions with the community

After creating a faction, you can assign units and lists to it from their respective builders.
